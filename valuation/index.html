Goal

Know what a company is worth.

Finance teams work it out by comparison: if similar companies sell for 17 times their yearly profit, this one probably does too. This method is called comps analysis.

Problem

The comparison lived in one Excel file, updated by hand.

Share prices and forecasts change all the time. Every change meant typing new numbers into the file.

Solution

A dashboard that pulls the figures in by itself.
