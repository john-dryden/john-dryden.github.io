Goal

Become fluent in German.

Problem

The more words you know, the rarer the next ones are.

Common words come up constantly, so they stick. Rare ones can go months without coming up.

Solution

A new story every day, written to include those rare words.

An automated system plants new and half-learned words, so they come up again and again.
