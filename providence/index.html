Goal

Know whether an AI system is good enough to use.

A company that puts AI to work, for example to sort its customer emails, first tests it on examples with known answers.

Problem

A score from a few examples can be luck.

11 of 12 correct is 92%. On 12 other examples, the same system could score much lower.

Solution

A tool that shows how far a score can be trusted.

It is called Providence.
